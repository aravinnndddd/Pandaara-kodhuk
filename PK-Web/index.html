True Story

HOW THIS STUPID IDEA STARTED

Date: 11 / 09 / 2026 • Somewhere in Kerala 🌴

The idea for Pandara Kothuk came from something I experienced on 11/09/2026.

I was just using my phone when a mosquito started annoying the hell out of me. It kept flying around, disturbing me, and I couldn't get rid of it.

At one point I was like:
“പണ്ടാരം പിടിച്ച കൊതുക്!” 😭

And then I thought...

“What if this mosquito could annoy me on my screen too?” 😂

And that's how Pandara Kothuk was born.

The idea was completely useless, but somehow, that was what made it fun.
